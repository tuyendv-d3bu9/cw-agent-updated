# Appeals — `shopgo-refund-v1`

1. **Deadline.** Submit an appeal within **5 working days** of receiving your results.
2. **Content.** For each point you appeal, give:
   - the criterion id (for example `A4b`, `A5a`, `I2`);
   - the evidence you believe was missed (file path in your frozen commit, and line or section);
   - your reasoning.
3. **Automated criteria (A1–A6).** The instructor re-runs the **same grader version** on the **same
   frozen commit**. Grading is deterministic, so the automated result is the same unless the grader has a
   defect.
4. **Decision record.** Every decision is recorded (appeal, evidence checked, decision, reviewer, date).
   Your result file is regenerated from the grader, never edited by hand.
5. **Grader defect.** If a defect in the grader is confirmed, a corrected grader version is released,
   **all** students are re-graded on the affected criterion, and the correction is announced.
6. **Instructor criteria (I1–I3).** An appeal on an instructor criterion is reviewed by the instructor
   and, when available, by a **second, independent reviewer** who scores it without seeing the first
   score. The final score is decided according to course policy.

Only the frozen commit is considered. New work pushed after the deadline cannot be used as evidence in an
appeal.
