# Business Requirements Document — Order Refund

Date: 2026-10-01 · Author: Nguyen Minh Duc (PO)

## Background
Today, ShopGo customers request refunds through the hotline; the Customer Service (CS) team handles them manually in a spreadsheet.
On average, CS receives about 1,200 refund requests per month.
Complaints about slow refunds made up 18% of all complaints in Q3/2026.

## Objectives
- Customers submit refund requests themselves on the ShopGo website.
- 90% of requests are processed within 2 working days.
- Automatically approve low-value requests to reduce the CS workload.

## Users
Customers who have purchased on the ShopGo website; CS agents and CS team leads.
