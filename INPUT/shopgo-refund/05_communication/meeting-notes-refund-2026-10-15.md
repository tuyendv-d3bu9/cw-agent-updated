# Meeting notes — Refund feature

Date: 2026-10-15 · Attendees: Nguyen Minh Duc (PO), Tran Thi Lan (BA), Pham Quoc Huy (Dev lead), Le Thu Ha (QA)

## Agreed items
1. Customers may not edit a refund request after submitting it; to change it, they cancel it and submit a new request.
2. Each order may have a refund request submitted at most 2 times, counting requests that were rejected or cancelled.
3. Customers receive a notification email when a request is approved or rejected.

## Open items
- Maximum time for CS to process one request: Huy and Lan to confirm later.
