# Change Request CR-07 — Refund request time limit

Date: 2026-10-20 · Requested by: Nguyen Minh Duc (PO) · Status: Approved

## Change
Changes rule R2 of the Refund SRS version 2.0: the time limit for submitting a refund request increases from 7 days to 10 days from the date of successful delivery.

## Reason
Reduce complaints from customers who receive their goods late during peak periods.

## Impact
The message displayed when the time limit has passed stays unchanged.
